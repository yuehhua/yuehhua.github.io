Featured research

Federated Learning

RAFAEL · Taiwan AI Labs 2023–2024

  • Federated learning
  • GWAS
  • Privacy-preserving computation

A federated framework for genome-wide association studies: secure multi-party computation with data never leaving its home institution. Its distributed linkage-disequilibrium algorithm, written in Julia, runs 3× faster single-threaded than the C++ gold-standard library.

500K participants
90M variants
<1 hr wall clock
3× faster LD (vs C++)
